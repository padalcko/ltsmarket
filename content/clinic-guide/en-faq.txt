## How much does it cost to open a laser hair removal studio?

The illustrative budget for a small studio using pre-owned equipment is PLN 60,000. Actual costs depend on equipment, premises and financing.

## Which PKD code applies to a beauty studio?

Under PKD 2025, beauty activities are classified under code 96.22.Z.

## Do I need to notify the sanitary authorities before opening?

There is no general notification obligation solely for starting cosmetic activities. Other premises and service-related requirements may apply.

## Can a cosmetologist perform laser hair removal?

This depends on the procedure, equipment intended purpose, operator qualifications and applicable regulations.

## Can I buy a pre-owned laser?

Yes, provided that its documentation, technical condition and maintenance history are properly verified.

## Can cosmetic lasers be financed through leasing?

Yes, subject to equipment eligibility and the financing provider's approval.

## How many treatments are required to break even?

With fixed costs of PLN 7,500, a treatment price of PLN 200 and variable costs of PLN 20, at least 42 treatments per month are required.

## Can a laser pay for itself within one year?

This is possible under certain financial scenarios, but depends on actual demand, expenses, taxation and financing.

## Does cosmetic laser equipment require CE marking?

Equipment must meet applicable conformity requirements. A CE label alone does not replace documentation verification.

## How often should a laser be serviced?

Service frequency depends on manufacturer recommendations, equipment design and operating intensity.

## Can a new beauty business receive financial support?

Businesses may apply for available programmes if they meet the eligibility criteria. Approval is not guaranteed.

## Does LTS Market help businesses select equipment?

LTS Market offers new and pre-owned equipment and professional equipment selection services according to its current offer.
