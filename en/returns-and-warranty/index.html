Returns, Complaints and Warranty

LTS Market supplies new and used aesthetic and cosmetology equipment and provides technical support and warranty service. Below you can find our terms for withdrawal from a contract, complaints, repairs and equipment replacement.

Equipment Warranty

New equipment sold by LTS Market comes with a 24-month commercial warranty. Used equipment comes with a 6-month commercial warranty.

Every used device undergoes technical diagnostics before sale and is delivered in working condition, as described in its product listing.

The commercial warranty is an additional commitment by the guarantor and does not limit or exclude the consumer's statutory rights relating to goods that do not conform to the contract.

Withdrawal from a Contract and Returns

Consumers who conclude a distance contract generally have the right to withdraw within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason, subject to applicable statutory exceptions.

To exercise this right, send an unequivocal withdrawal statement to sales@ltsmarket.pl before the deadline. Following withdrawal, the goods must be returned or handed over within 14 days of submitting the statement.

Consumers may inspect equipment to the extent necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning. Use beyond what is necessary for this inspection may result in liability for any reduction in the value of the goods under applicable law.

Refunds are processed within statutory deadlines and in accordance with the rules governing original delivery costs and the seller's right to withhold reimbursement until the goods or evidence of their return have been received.

Consumers bear the direct cost of ordinary return shipping where they were properly informed before purchase and the law permits this. For equipment that cannot normally be returned by post due to its nature, information about return costs must be provided before the contract is concluded.

For B2B transactions, LTS Market does not offer voluntary returns of functioning equipment without an individual agreement. This does not affect mandatory statutory rights, including those of sole traders who qualify for consumer-like protection under Polish law.

Complaints and Warranty Repairs

If equipment develops a fault, please contact LTS Market with purchase details, the device model and a description of the issue.

Under the commercial warranty, the device will undergo technical assessment. If the fault is covered, we will arrange a repair. If repair is not possible, equipment replacement will be considered in accordance with the warranty terms.

Independently of the commercial warranty, consumers retain their statutory rights where goods do not conform to the contract, including remedies involving repair, replacement, price reduction or withdrawal where provided by law.

Equipment Replacement

Replacement may be provided for a confirmed warranty-covered fault that cannot be effectively repaired, in accordance with the warranty terms.

These provisions do not restrict any statutory right to replacement available under applicable law.

Damage Not Covered by the Commercial Warranty

The commercial warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, impact, dropping the device, a broken display, liquid ingress, improper power supply, voltage surges or unauthorised modifications where those circumstances caused the reported fault.

Each case is assessed individually based on the established cause of the failure. Signs of use or external damage do not automatically remove all of the buyer's statutory rights.

Transport and Delivery of Equipment

Equipment may be sent by courier, delivered in person by prior arrangement, or collected by LTS Market following an agreed collection arrangement.

How to Submit a Complaint

Email sales@ltsmarket.pl with your order number or other proof of purchase, device name and model, a description of the fault and, if available, photographs or video showing the issue.

Phone: +48 575 254 431.

No specific form is required to submit a complaint. Complaints are handled under applicable law, including statutory deadlines for responding to consumer complaints.
