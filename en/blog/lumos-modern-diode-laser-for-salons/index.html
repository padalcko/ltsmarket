Lumos: a modern diode laser for hair removal salons

A modern salon needs equipment that supports its team and room to develop its services. Lumos in the LTS Market catalogue combines four wavelengths with digital features and extensive equipment options. Together with Laser Tech Service support, it is a proposition for clinics that view their device as a long-term investment.

Keeping pace with the modern salon

A device’s modernity is best judged by practical value: configuration options, ease of use and planned technical support. Lumos brings treatment functions together with Android, CRM and a dermatoscope listed in our offer. That combination makes it worth considering for salons developing both their technology and workflow.

The model also appears in ALIS Laser’s innovation overview, discussing hair removal devices with handpiece power of at least 2000 W. Always check the ordered version against its own documentation.

Four wavelengths and a broad setting range

Our Lumos catalogue entry lists 755, 808, 940 and 1064 nm, handpiece power of 2000–4000 W, energy of 10–240 J, frequency of 1–30 Hz and pulses from 5 ms. These ranges cover the configurations on offer; confirm the specifications of the version you order.

This gives salon owners a basis for choosing equipment to suit their plans. Four wavelengths do not guarantee results for every hair colour or skin type. Treatment selection and settings should follow the device instructions and the operator’s training.

Features to explore during a demonstration

The catalogue lists ICE Flow, FAC, NeoDez, a dermatoscope, CRM and Android. A demonstration is an opportunity to look beyond feature names: try the screen, available settings, handpiece handling and digital modules.

Assess whether the equipment fits the team’s actual workflow. Confirm CRM scope, software updates and module functions for the chosen configuration. Automatic integrations or diagnostic capabilities should not be assumed without documentation.

Laser Tech Service: support beyond the purchase

Technical support contributes to the value of Lumos. You can plan its servicing with Laser Tech Service, whose offer covers diode lasers, diagnostics, handpieces, cooling and electronics. Explore Laser Tech Service.

A clear contact route matters when a salon experiences a fault. Agree maintenance intervals, support scope, reporting arrangements and warranty terms when selecting Lumos. Planned maintenance helps organise salon operations; it does not promise fault-free use or a fixed repair time.

Documentation and certification for use in Poland

Lumos supplied by LTS Market has the complete documentation and certification required for it to be placed on the market and used for its intended purpose in Poland. This is an important part of its value for salons developing their services with documented equipment provenance and conformity.

The documents are not published online. Documentation for the specific configuration can be discussed directly with LTS Market when preparing a quotation. Certification does not replace operator training, treatment suitability assessment or compliance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

Investment value: equipment, introduction and upkeep

The LTS Market catalogue lists Lumos at PLN 68,000–100,000 depending on configuration. Confirm the current quotation and what is included. Compare equipment options, team preparation, support arrangements and operating costs.

Lumos can be an optimal modern choice for a salon that needs an extensive platform and will use its capabilities. Suitability depends on treatment volume, budget and team skills. Value goes beyond maximum advertised power, while returns also depend on costs and bookings.

Free operator training: preparing your team

LTS Market provides free operator training. Buying Lumos therefore also gives your team an opportunity to learn how to operate the device and discuss questions before starting work. Training helps operators understand the equipment and use its capabilities in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

We agree the training schedule and programme at purchase. For the salon owner, this means support when introducing the equipment, with no additional charge for operator training.

Buy a new device and receive a salon website for one year

Salons purchasing Lumos or any other new device from LTS Market receive a complimentary brochure website for 1 year. For the first 3 months, we provide free website support and promotion. After that period, continued support and promotion are available at substantial discounts.

This complements the equipment investment: the salon also gains its own online destination for people interested in treatments. We discuss website delivery details and the terms of continued cooperation individually.

How can a salon website help attract clients?

A brochure website brings the salon, its treatments, equipment and team together in one place. It can explain services, answer common questions and present the address, opening hours and contact details. Prospective clients can more easily find the information they need to get in touch and plan a visit.

The website link can be used on social media, a Google Business Profile, printed materials and advertising campaigns. This gives the salon a consistent destination for promotional activity and a foundation for developing visibility in local search results.

A clear presentation helps build trust and makes it easier to move from interest to a treatment enquiry. The website and its promotion can support client acquisition; results also depend on the local market, the appeal of the offer and how enquiries are handled.
