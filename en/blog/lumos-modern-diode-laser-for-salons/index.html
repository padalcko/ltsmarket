Why Lumos deserves your attention

Choosing a professional laser hair removal system is a major investment for any beauty salon or aesthetic clinic.

Today's business owners need more than impressive power specifications. They need flexible treatment settings, reliable cooling, intuitive controls, organised client information and dependable technical support.

Lumos brings these features together in one professional platform.

The system combines four wavelengths, handpiece configurations rated from 2000 to 4000 W, ICE Flow cooling, FAC optics, Android-based controls and CRM functionality.

A dermatoscope is also listed among its available equipment options.

In our assessment, this combination makes Lumos one of the more attractive options in its category when comparing equipment, functionality, price and after-sales support.

Four wavelengths for greater flexibility

Lumos combines four wavelengths: 755, 808, 940 and 1064 nm.

755 nm offers relatively high melanin absorption. It can be useful in appropriately selected treatments, depending on skin phototype and hair characteristics.

808 nm is one of the most widely used wavelengths in professional diode laser hair removal systems. It is a well-established part of diode laser technology.

940 nm adds another wavelength to the system, expanding its available configuration options. Its application should follow the manufacturer's protocols.

1064 nm has lower melanin absorption than shorter wavelengths. Under appropriate treatment conditions, it may also be useful for darker skin phototypes.

The presence of four wavelengths provides flexibility, but it does not guarantee identical results for every client.

Professional assessment, appropriate settings and trained operators remain essential.

Power from 2000 to 4000 W

Lumos is available with handpiece power configurations ranging from 2000 to 4000 W.

The advertised energy range is 10–240 J, pulse frequency is 1–30 Hz, and pulse duration starts from 5 ms.

These specifications provide a broad range of configuration possibilities.

However, maximum power alone does not determine treatment performance.

Optical design, energy delivery, cooling, equipment condition and operator technique are equally important.

LTS Market helps clients select a configuration that matches their business requirements rather than focusing only on the highest available power rating.

ICE Flow cooling technology

Temperature management is an essential part of professional diode laser operation.

Lumos uses ICE Flow technology to support cooling during treatments.

Appropriate cooling conditions are important for client comfort and the correct operation of the handpiece.

The cooling system also requires regular maintenance.

Coolant condition, circulation and component cleanliness should be checked according to the manufacturer's instructions.

This is one reason why professional technical support matters when choosing laser equipment.

FAC optical technology

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation.

It is an optical technology used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along one axis.

Optical quality is an important element of laser design, alongside diode performance, cooling and control electronics.

Lumos incorporates FAC as part of its optical technology.

NeoDez and equipment hygiene

Maintaining proper hygiene is essential in every professional beauty clinic.

Lumos offers NeoDez, a technology associated with additional hygiene-related functions of the handpiece.

The exact capabilities depend on the device configuration and manufacturer documentation.

NeoDez should be regarded as a supplementary feature, not a replacement for standard cleaning and disinfection procedures.

Integrated CRM — less paperwork, better organisation

Imagine a returning client arriving several weeks after their previous appointment.

The operator needs to review previous procedures, recorded settings and relevant treatment history.

In many salons, this information is scattered across paper forms, notebooks or separate computer files.

Lumos offers CRM functionality designed to help organise client information.

Depending on the software version, the system may support client profiles, treatment history and stored settings.

Where automatic restoration of previous settings is supported, preparing for a returning client may become even more convenient.

The operator must still review and approve all treatment parameters.

The practical benefit is straightforward: fewer paper notes, more organised information and easier access to client history.

Before purchasing, salon owners should request a CRM demonstration and confirm the available data protection features.

Android interface and software updates

Lumos uses an Android-based control interface.

This provides a modern environment for accessing equipment settings and digital functions.

Software maintenance is another important consideration.

Updates may improve stability, correct software issues and introduce refinements to existing features.

The availability and installation method of updates, including any automatic update functionality, depend on the installed software version and manufacturer support.

Before purchasing, it is worth confirming how updates are delivered and what support period applies.

Dermatoscope — an additional professional tool

A dermatoscope is listed among the available Lumos equipment options.

It provides additional capabilities for visual skin observation within the limits defined by the manufacturer.

Depending on the installed software and equipment, it may support consultation workflows and the documentation of observations.

It does not replace medical dermatological examinations or diagnosis.

For a professional beauty business, it represents another example of integrating multiple useful functions into one platform.
