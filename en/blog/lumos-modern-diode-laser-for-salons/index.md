## Four wavelengths for greater flexibility

Lumos combines four wavelengths: 755, 808, 940 and 1064 nm.

**755 nm** offers relatively high melanin absorption. It can be useful in appropriately selected treatments, depending on skin phototype and hair characteristics.

**808 nm** is one of the most widely used wavelengths in professional diode laser hair removal systems. It is a well-established part of diode laser technology.

**940 nm** adds another wavelength to the system, expanding its available configuration options. Its application should follow the manufacturer's protocols.

**1064 nm** has lower melanin absorption than shorter wavelengths. Under appropriate treatment conditions, it may also be useful for darker skin phototypes.

The presence of four wavelengths provides flexibility, but it does not guarantee identical results for every client.

Professional assessment, appropriate settings and trained operators remain essential.

## FAC optical technology

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation.

It is an optical technology used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along one axis.

Optical quality is an important element of laser design, alongside diode performance, cooling and control electronics.

Lumos incorporates FAC as part of its optical technology.
