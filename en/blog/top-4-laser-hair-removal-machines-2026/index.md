## What makes laser hair removal machines different?

Laser hair removal is based on the principle of selective photothermolysis. Laser radiation is absorbed by melanin and converted into thermal energy, which affects structures within the hair follicle.

However, treatment effectiveness depends on more than the laser type. Pulse parameters, individual skin and hair characteristics, and practitioner training also play important roles.

### Wavelength

Illustrative diagram of how different laser wavelengths propagate through the skin. It does not represent measured penetration depths or clinical effectiveness.

Professional laser hair removal systems use wavelengths including the following:

| Wavelength | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| 755 nm | Relatively high melanin absorption |
| 808–810 nm | A balance between melanin absorption and tissue penetration |
| 940 nm | An additional wavelength used in selected multi-wavelength systems |
| 1064 nm | Lower epidermal melanin absorption and a tendency towards deeper penetration |

Multi-wavelength diode systems expand the technical capabilities of laser equipment. However, the number of wavelengths alone does not guarantee greater treatment effectiveness.

A diode laser emitting at 755 nm should not be confused with an alexandrite laser operating at the same wavelength.

### Fluence and pulse duration

Fluence, measured in J/cm², describes the amount of energy delivered per unit area of skin.

Pulse duration influences how tissues are heated. Adjustable parameters allow appropriately trained practitioners to adapt treatments to skin phototype, hair thickness and other individual characteristics.

### Treatment spot size

Treatment spot size influences the speed of treating larger body areas and the precision required when working on smaller areas.

A larger treatment spot may reduce treatment time, while smaller applicators can improve accessibility and precision in more demanding treatment areas.

### Cooling systems

Cooling is one of the most important components of professional laser hair removal equipment. It helps limit excessive heating of superficial skin layers and reduce treatment discomfort.

Modern manufacturers use cryogen cooling, contact sapphire cooling, air cooling and various combinations of these technologies.

### 1. Candela GentleLase PRO

![Candela GentleLase PRO alexandrite laser](https://ltsmarket.pl/assets/img/blog/top-4-laser-hair-removal-2026/candela-gentlelase-pro.webp)

Candela GentleLase PRO is one of the most recognisable examples of professional alexandrite laser technology.

The device operates at a wavelength of 755 nm, which is characterised by relatively high melanin absorption.

One of its distinctive features is the Dynamic Cooling Device (DCD). The system delivers a controlled amount of cryogen to the skin surface before the laser pulse.

This approach allows skin cooling without the need for contact gel when using the appropriate operating configuration.

Key features:

- Alexandrite laser technology.

- 755 nm wavelength.

- DCD cooling system.

- Multiple treatment spot size options.

GentleLase PRO remains a recognisable example of professional equipment based on established alexandrite laser technology.

### 2. Lumenis LightSheer Duet

One of its distinctive features is the use of two different treatment handpieces: ET and HS.

The ET handpiece incorporates ChillTip contact cooling. The HS handpiece uses vacuum-assisted technology and a larger treatment area designed, among other applications, for treating larger body regions.

This approach allows practitioners to select the appropriate handpiece according to the treatment area and their workflow.

Key features:

- Diode laser technology.

- Approximately 805 nm wavelength.

LightSheer Duet illustrates how handpiece design and energy delivery methods can influence treatment workflow and duration.

### 3. Lumos

![Lumos professional diode laser](https://ltsmarket.pl/assets/img/lumos/lumos-front-800.webp)

Lumos represents a modern direction in the development of multi-wavelength diode laser systems, combining advanced laser modules, optical technology, cooling systems and digital tools.

The device is available in configurations with a declared handpiece power ranging from 2000 to 4000 W. Its pulse repetition frequency ranges from 1 to 30 Hz, with pulse durations starting at 5 ms.

One of the optical technologies incorporated into Lumos is FAC (Fast Axis Collimation). This technology reduces the divergence of radiation emitted by laser diodes along the fast axis, supporting improved control of the laser beam.

The ICE Flow cooling system helps maintain appropriate thermal conditions during equipment operation.

Another notable feature is the availability of a digital dermatoscope in selected configurations. It allows practitioners to observe the skin surface and hair under magnification and document visible changes throughout a course of treatments.

Key features:

- Multi-wavelength diode laser technology.

- Declared handpiece power from 2000 to 4000 W, depending on configuration.

- Pulse repetition frequency of 1–30 Hz.

- Pulse duration starting at 5 ms.

- FAC optical technology.

- ICE Flow cooling system.

- Digital dermatoscope in selected configurations.

- Professional technical service support in Poland.

Lumos demonstrates a modern approach to professional aesthetic equipment, combining laser technology with additional tools that support practitioners in their daily work.

## Which laser hair removal machine should you choose?

There is no universal device that represents the best solution for every aesthetic clinic and every client.

Alexandrite systems remain an important part of professional laser hair removal. Diode platforms continue to evolve through improvements in optics, cooling systems, handpiece design and digital technologies.

Very light, grey and white hair may respond poorly to laser hair removal due to its limited melanin content. None of the technologies presented guarantees completely painless treatments or the elimination of adverse reaction risks.

When choosing equipment, business owners should consider not only technical specifications but also operating costs, spare parts availability, warranty conditions and access to professional technical support.

Even the most advanced laser cannot generate revenue while it remains out of service awaiting repairs.
