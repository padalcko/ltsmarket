Alpha Mini. Professional technology in a compact design

Starting a beauty salon does not mean you need to invest in the most expensive equipment immediately.

For smaller clinics and new businesses, Alpha Mini offers a practical introduction to professional laser hair removal.

The device features a handpiece with 1000–1200 W of power and an ICE Flow cooling system that helps manage heat during treatments.

Its standard treatment window measures 12 × 12 mm, while the handpiece includes a touchscreen for convenient parameter adjustments.

Another important advantage is its compact construction. With a weight below 35 kg, Alpha Mini is easier to accommodate in smaller treatment rooms.

This model is particularly interesting for new beauty businesses, independent cosmetologists and established salons introducing laser hair removal as an additional service.

Alpha Mini prices start at PLN 40,000.

Alpha Diode Laser. More power for growing clinics

Now imagine a busy laser hair removal studio with a full appointment schedule and clients arriving throughout the day.

For this type of business, the Alpha Diode Laser deserves attention.

Alpha offers 1600–2000 W of handpiece power and a larger 12 × 20 mm treatment window. The larger window covers more skin with each placement of the handpiece.

The device also features TEC 1200 W cooling and FAC optical technology.

But Alpha is not simply about higher power.

Its additional features are particularly interesting for clinics looking to improve their daily workflow.

A built-in dermatoscope provides an additional tool for observing skin and hair. The CRM system helps organise client information, while Android software supports the device interface.

Automatic parameter selection can assist the operator when preparing treatment settings. Final parameters must always be confirmed by a properly trained professional.

Alpha is a strong candidate for businesses planning to make laser hair removal one of their main services.

Alpha prices start at PLN 60,000.

Which laser should you choose?

Choose Alpha Mini if you are opening a salon, working with limited space or introducing laser hair removal as a new service.

Consider Alpha if your clinic already has an established client base, expects a higher treatment workload or would benefit from its additional features.

The best choice is not necessarily the most expensive device. It is the one that fits your business.
