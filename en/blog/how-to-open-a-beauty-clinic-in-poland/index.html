How to Open an Aesthetic Beauty Business in Poland in 2027

26.11.2026 · Oleksandr Padalko — Specialist in digital business development, search visibility and AI search optimization.

Starting an aesthetic beauty business in Poland requires a business plan, business registration, suitable premises, compliance with hygiene requirements and equipment appropriate for the intended treatments.

In the illustrative model presented in this guide, a small laser hair removal studio using pre-owned equipment requires an initial budget of approximately PLN 60,000. A studio using new equipment is represented by a PLN 126,000 investment scenario.

These figures are planning examples, not official market averages or guaranteed startup costs.

Key takeaways

  • PKD code 96.22.Z covers beauty services, including non-medical hair removal.
  • Treatments must be consistent with staff qualifications, equipment intended purpose and applicable regulations.
  • Equipment may be purchased new, pre-owned or financed through leasing.
  • Startup budgets should include servicing, training, marketing and financial reserves.
  • Profitability depends on treatment volume, pricing and operating expenses.
  • Equipment documentation and technical condition should be verified before purchase.

Where should you start when opening a beauty business?

Start by defining your services, target customers and available budget.

Purchasing equipment before developing a business plan can leave insufficient funds for premises, marketing, training and the first months of operation.

Assess local competition, treatment prices, customer demand, seasonal fluctuations, staffing requirements and equipment operating costs.

A small studio specialising in laser hair removal may be easier to manage than a multi-service business with several expensive devices and high fixed costs.

How do you register a beauty business in Poland?

Common business structures include a sole proprietorship, known as JDG, and a limited liability company, known as Sp. z o.o.

Sole proprietorships are registered through CEIDG, while limited liability companies are registered through KRS.

The chosen structure affects liability, taxation, accounting and operating expenses.

Before registration, determine the appropriate PKD code, taxation method and obligations relating to ZUS, VAT, fiscal cash registers and invoicing.

Consult Biznes.gov.pl and a qualified accountant for current requirements.

Which PKD code applies to a beauty studio in 2027?

Under the PKD 2025 classification, beauty care and other cosmetic activities are classified under code 96.22.Z.

This includes cosmetic services such as non-medical hair removal.

The general transitional period for the previous PKD 2007 classification ends on 31 December 2026.

A business classification code does not automatically authorise every type of laser treatment.

Can a cosmetologist perform laser treatments in Poland?

Whether a particular procedure can be performed depends on its nature, equipment intended purpose, operator qualifications and applicable regulations.

Cosmetic laser hair removal must be distinguished from aesthetic and restorative medical procedures.

In its communication dated 23 January 2026, the Polish Ministry of Health presented its position on aesthetic and restorative medical procedures performed by appropriately qualified physicians.

This does not automatically classify every laser application as a medical procedure. Nor does purchasing a laser authorise an operator to perform every possible treatment.

Check the manufacturer's documentation and current legal requirements before introducing a new service.

What sanitary requirements apply to a beauty studio?

A beauty studio must provide safe and hygienic conditions for the services it offers.

According to Poland's Chief Sanitary Inspectorate, there is no general obligation to notify the sanitary authorities solely because a cosmetic business is starting operations.

However, requirements relating to the premises, change of use, adaptation, technical conditions and hygiene procedures may still apply.

Before signing a lease, assess water access, ventilation, electrical installations, cleaning and disinfection arrangements, material storage and waste management.

Specific requirements depend on the services provided.

How much does it cost to rent a beauty studio?

Rental costs depend on the city, floor area, condition of the premises and whether the business rents an independent unit or a treatment room within an existing salon.

For an illustrative budget, you may assume PLN 1,400–2,350 monthly rent. This is neither a market average nor a verified listing survey. Deposits, utility bills, administrative charges and adaptation costs should also be included.

What equipment should you buy for your first beauty studio?

Equipment should match the intended services, staff qualifications and available budget.

Evaluate handpiece parameters, energy stability, cooling performance, ergonomics, documentation, warranty conditions, spare parts and technical service availability.

Is buying a pre-owned cosmetic laser a good option?

A pre-owned laser can reduce initial investment if its documentation, technical condition and maintenance history are properly verified.

The pulse counter is not the only indicator of equipment wear.

The laser module, energy stability, cooling system, repair history and availability of spare parts should also be assessed.

What documentation should a cosmetic laser have?

Equipment documentation should establish its origin, intended purpose and compliance with applicable requirements.

Commission Implementing Regulation EU 2022/2347 classifies certain non-medical-purpose equipment using high-intensity electromagnetic radiation exclusively for hair removal as Class IIa.

Before purchasing, verify the EU declaration of conformity, CE marking, conformity assessment documentation, instructions, manufacturer details and serial number, taking applicable transitional provisions into account.

A CE label alone is not a substitute for reviewing the documentation.

For pre-owned equipment, maintenance history and technical condition should also be assessed.

Can you finance beauty equipment through leasing?

Leasing can reduce the initial cash expenditure required to purchase equipment, but it creates recurring financial commitments.

Availability depends on the equipment, the business owner's financial circumstances and the financing provider's decision.

Compare the initial payment, monthly instalments, contract duration, purchase option, insurance and total financing cost.

What are the monthly operating costs of a beauty studio?

Typical operating expenses include rent, utilities, ZUS contributions, marketing, accounting, insurance, consumables and a maintenance reserve.

In 2026, standard full social insurance contributions amounted to PLN 1,926.76 per month under the applicable standard assumptions. Preferential social insurance contributions amounted to PLN 456.18.

Health insurance contributions are calculated separately.

The applicable 2027 figures should be checked once officially published.

From 2026, the general Polish VAT exemption threshold is PLN 240,000 in annual turnover, subject to statutory conditions and exceptions.

The illustrative financial model in this guide assumes PLN 7,500 in monthly fixed costs.

Is a laser hair removal business profitable?

Profitability depends on treatment volume, average treatment price, variable costs and monthly operating expenses.

At an average treatment price of PLN 200, three treatments per day and 22 working days per month, monthly revenue is PLN 13,200.

With variable costs of PLN 20 per treatment and fixed costs of PLN 7,500, the operating surplus is PLN 4,380 before taxes, financing and owner withdrawals.

This is an illustrative calculation rather than a guaranteed income forecast.

How many treatments are needed to break even?

The operating break-even point is calculated by dividing monthly fixed costs by the difference between treatment price and variable cost per treatment.

With fixed costs of PLN 7,500, a treatment price of PLN 200 and variable costs of PLN 20, at least 42 treatments per month are needed to cover operating expenses.

If the owner also requires PLN 4,000 per month for personal withdrawals, at least 64 treatments are needed before taxes and other obligations.

How long does it take to recover the equipment investment?

The payback period depends on the investment amount and the actual cash remaining after operating expenses.

With an initial investment of PLN 60,000 and three treatments per day, the operating surplus is PLN 4,380. After a PLN 4,000 owner withdrawal, PLN 380 remains before taxes and financing.

The simplified payback period is approximately 158 months.

At five treatments per day, with the remaining assumptions unchanged, the operating surplus increases to PLN 12,300. After the owner's withdrawal, PLN 8,300 remains, resulting in a simplified payback period of approximately 7.2 months.

These scenarios demonstrate why realistic customer demand estimates are essential.

Why should technical servicing be included in the business plan?

Equipment failure creates repair expenses, cancelled appointments and lost revenue.

At five treatments per day and an average price of PLN 200, one day of downtime represents PLN 1,000 in potential lost revenue.

Regular maintenance can help identify cooling, power supply and handpiece problems before they develop into more serious failures.

How should staff be trained?

Staff must have qualifications appropriate to the procedures they perform and understand the manufacturer's operating instructions.

Training should cover safety, contraindications, eye protection, workstation preparation, equipment faults and treatment documentation.

Product-specific training does not replace qualifications required by law.

How can a new beauty studio attract its first customers?

Marketing should ideally begin before the studio opens.

Useful channels include Google Business Profile, a dedicated website, appointment booking platforms, social media and local partnerships.

Marketing claims should be accurate and should not guarantee treatment outcomes that cannot be substantiated.

A useful website should answer questions about technology, pricing, safety, treatment preparation and contraindications.

Conclusion

Opening a beauty studio requires financial planning, suitable equipment, appropriate qualifications and compliance with applicable regulations.

Equipment selection should be part of a broader business plan that includes maintenance, financing, operating expenses and realistic customer acquisition expectations.
