Contact form

The form prepares a draft in your email app. Filling it in does not submit an enquiry to LTS Market. You must send the message in your email app. The draft includes the name, company, email, phone, subject and message you entered. Provide only information needed to answer your enquiry. Your email provider’s policies also apply to email handling.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics”. It is used to understand visits and website usage. The service may process browser, device and page information and store cookies. You can refuse or withdraw consent through “Privacy settings” in the footer. Withdrawal stops further analytics in this browser; it does not erase information already sent to the provider.

Saving your choice

Your analytics choice is saved locally in this browser under lts-analytics-consent-v1. You can change it in the website privacy settings or remove it by clearing this website’s browser data.

Data enquiries

Send questions about data processing, retention or requests concerning your personal data to sales@ltsmarket.pl.
