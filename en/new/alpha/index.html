TEC 1200 W Cooling for Professional Operation

Cooling is an important part of diode laser design. It helps maintain suitable operating conditions for components during regular use.

Alpha features a TEC cooling system rated at 1200 W. Thermoelectric cooling technology manages heat transfer within the system.

Regular maintenance and technical inspections help keep the cooling system in suitable operating condition.

FAC Optical Technology

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation. It is an optical technique used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along one axis.

The optical design helps shape and direct laser emission through the device.

FAC is one of the technologies used in Alpha alongside its diode handpiece, cooling system and electronic controls.
