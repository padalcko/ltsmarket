## FAC Optical Technology

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation. It is an optical technique used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along one axis.

The optical design helps shape and direct laser emission through the device.

FAC is one of the technologies used in Alpha alongside its diode handpiece, cooling system and electronic controls.
