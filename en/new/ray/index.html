1200 W Handpiece and 150 J Energy

When choosing a professional diode laser, it is important to consider not only output power but also optics, cooling, energy delivery and handpiece ergonomics.

Treatment parameters must be selected according to the manufacturer's instructions and professional client assessment.

12-Bar Diode Stack

Ray uses a diode stack consisting of 12 laser bars.

A laser bar is a semiconductor component containing laser emitters. Multiple bars form part of the light-generating system inside the handpiece.

Correct electrical control, cooling and component condition are essential for proper operation.

The number of laser bars should not be treated as a direct measure of treatment effectiveness.

FAC Optical Technology

Ray incorporates Fast Axis Collimation, an optical technique used to reduce laser beam divergence along the fast axis.

The optical system helps shape and direct the emission generated by the diode stack.

Maintaining the optical components in suitable condition is an important part of professional equipment care.

Freon 300 W Cooling

Ray is listed with a cooling system described as Freon 300 W.

The cooling system supports suitable operating conditions for the equipment's components.

Refrigeration systems require correct maintenance, including attention to system integrity, heat exchange and component condition.

The exact refrigerant and cooling-system design should be confirmed in the documentation for the supplied device.
