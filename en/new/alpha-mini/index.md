## Alpha Mini laser hair removal technology

Selective photothermolysis relies on melanin in the hair absorbing light. Pulse energy becomes heat that acts on structures in the hair follicle. The operator selects settings for the skin, hair and treatment area; results and sensations vary between individuals.

Available wavelength variants are 808 nm; 755 + 808 + 1064 nm; and 755 + 808 + 940 + 1064 nm. These are alternative handpiece configurations, rather than switchable features of a single set. The base configuration and options for the offer starting at PLN 40,000 are agreed during consultation.

## ICE Flow cooling system

The system combines contact cooling through a sapphire window, thermoelectric TEC elements, a liquid circuit and air heat dissipation. Skin cooling helps reduce discomfort, while component cooling supports stable laser operation.

The LTS Market product card specifies TEC 500 W. Regular inspection of the cooling circuit, filters and fluid is part of maintenance. Follow the supplied configuration’s manual and adjust the maintenance schedule to usage.

## Handpiece and intelligent features

Handpiece power is 1000–1200 W, depending on configuration. The standard treatment window is 12 × 12 mm; a 12 × 20 mm option can be agreed when ordering. The handpiece has a 2-inch touchscreen.

Handpiece controls allow adjustments to energy, frequency, pulse duration and cooling without reaching for the main panel. The grip supports handling across the treatment area.

Available configurations include Android, CRM for customer and treatment records, error self-checks and software updates. Equipment options also include a dermatoscope with 50–200× magnification and an additional mode for acne-prone skin treatments.

Availability of these functions in your LTS Market set is confirmed before purchase. They are not automatically included as base equipment in the offer starting at PLN 40,000. Applications and settings must follow the device manual.
