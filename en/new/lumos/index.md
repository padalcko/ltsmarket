## Four Wavelengths in One Diode Laser System

Different wavelengths interact with melanin and tissue in different ways. Combining four wavelengths gives trained operators more flexibility when selecting appropriate treatment parameters.

### 755 nm — High Melanin Absorption

The 755 nm wavelength has relatively high melanin absorption. It may be used in appropriately selected procedures based on skin phototype and hair characteristics.

### 808 nm — Established Diode Laser Technology

The 808 nm wavelength is widely used in professional diode laser hair removal systems and is commonly associated with reducing unwanted hair growth.

### 940 nm — Additional Configuration Flexibility

The 940 nm wavelength extends the range of available options in a multi-wavelength system. Its use should follow the manufacturer's treatment protocols.

### 1064 nm — Different Absorption Characteristics

The 1064 nm wavelength has lower melanin absorption than shorter wavelengths and may be used in appropriately selected procedures, including those involving darker skin phototypes.

Four wavelengths do not eliminate the need for professional client assessment, correct parameter selection or operator training.

## FAC Technology — Optical Beam Control

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation, an optical technique used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along the fast axis.
