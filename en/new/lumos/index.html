Lumos — More Than Power. Technology for Professional Work.

Professional laser hair removal equipment should be evaluated on more than maximum output power. Adjustable treatment parameters, operating stability, cooling and ease of use all contribute to the experience of working with a laser in a busy clinic.

Lumos combines four wavelengths — 755, 808, 940 and 1064 nm — in a professional diode laser system. Handpiece options ranging from 2000 to 4000 W, energy settings of 10–240 J and frequencies up to 30 Hz provide a broad range of operating parameters within the manufacturer's intended procedures.

The system also offers technologies such as ICE Flow cooling, FAC optics, NeoDez and Android-based software with CRM functionality.

Four Wavelengths in One Diode Laser System

Different wavelengths interact with melanin and tissue in different ways. Combining four wavelengths gives trained operators more flexibility when selecting appropriate treatment parameters.

755 nm — High Melanin Absorption

The 755 nm wavelength has relatively high melanin absorption. It may be used in appropriately selected procedures based on skin phototype and hair characteristics.

808 nm — Established Diode Laser Technology

The 808 nm wavelength is widely used in professional diode laser hair removal systems and is commonly associated with reducing unwanted hair growth.

940 nm — Additional Configuration Flexibility

The 940 nm wavelength extends the range of available options in a multi-wavelength system. Its use should follow the manufacturer's treatment protocols.

1064 nm — Different Absorption Characteristics

The 1064 nm wavelength has lower melanin absorption than shorter wavelengths and may be used in appropriately selected procedures, including those involving darker skin phototypes.

Four wavelengths do not eliminate the need for professional client assessment, correct parameter selection or operator training.

FAC Technology — Optical Beam Control

FAC stands for Fast Axis Collimation, an optical technique used in diode laser systems to reduce beam divergence along the fast axis.

A properly designed optical system helps shape and direct laser emission through the device's optical path.

Optical quality is one of several important aspects of professional laser construction, alongside diode performance, cooling and electronic control.

NeoDez — Additional Hygiene Technology

Maintaining clean treatment equipment is an essential part of professional salon operation.

Lumos offers NeoDez technology, associated with additional hygiene functions for the treatment handpiece.

NeoDez is intended to complement, rather than replace, standard cleaning and disinfection procedures. Operators must continue to follow the manufacturer's hygiene instructions and applicable salon procedures.

The precise functions depend on the selected Lumos configuration.
