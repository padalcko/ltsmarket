INDIVIDUAL APPROACH

We do not push one leasing option. We look for the right solution for you.

Every business is different. Company history, available budget, equipment value and financial situation all vary from client to client.

That is why LTS Market does not rely on one fixed financing model. We first understand your needs and then help find a solution suited to your purchase and business situation.

We serve clients throughout Poland and cooperate with banks and leasing companies, allowing you to focus on choosing the right equipment instead of starting your financing search from scratch.

WHY LEASING?

Develop your business without financing the entire purchase at once.

Professional beauty equipment can represent a significant investment. Leasing may allow you to spread the cost over time and retain more company capital for day-to-day operations, marketing, staff and further business development.

The final conditions, initial payment, financing period and monthly payment are determined individually.

Financing conditions depend on factors including the equipment, transaction value, business situation and the decision of the financing institution.
