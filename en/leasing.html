FINANCING OPTIONS

Choose the right type of leasing

Operating lease

A financing option for businesses that want to use professional equipment without paying its entire purchase price at the beginning of the investment. The financing institution retains ownership during the lease term, while buyout conditions are specified in the agreement.

Finance lease

A financing option for businesses under which the leased equipment is generally treated as an asset of the lessee for tax purposes. Payment arrangements and ownership transfer conditions depend on the agreement and applicable legislation.

Consumer leasing

A financing option for private individuals who do not operate a registered business. Availability depends on the customer's individual assessment and the financing institution's offer.

APPLICATION PROCESS

What documents are required for leasing?

The required documentation depends on the type of financing, the equipment value and the financing institution's application procedure.

Business applicants may be asked to provide:

  • Polish tax identification number (NIP) and business registration details.
  • Identification document of the authorised company representative.
  • Leasing application.
  • Equipment quotation or pro forma invoice.
  • Additional business financial documents, if requested.

Consumer leasing applicants may be required to provide identification, income information and additional documents necessary for a financial assessment.

LTS Market helps prepare equipment documentation and assists customers throughout the application process.

The final list of required documents is determined individually.

Frequently asked questions about beauty equipment leasing

Can I lease a hair removal laser in Poland?

Yes. LTS Market helps arrange financing for new hair removal lasers and professional beauty equipment throughout Poland.

What is the minimum down payment?

The minimum down payment is 10% of the equipment value. The final amount depends on the customer's individual assessment and financing conditions.

What leasing periods are available?

Financing is available for periods ranging from 24 to 60 months. The payment schedule is determined individually.

Can a newly established business apply for leasing?

Yes. Businesses that have been operating for at least three months may apply. Approval depends on the financing institution's decision.

Can a private individual lease professional beauty equipment?

Yes. Private individuals without a registered business may apply for consumer leasing. Availability depends on their individual financial assessment.

Can foreign entrepreneurs obtain leasing in Poland?

Yes, financing may be available to foreign entrepreneurs. Requirements depend on the applicant's legal status, documentation and the financing institution's procedures.

Can I lease a pre-owned hair removal laser?

No. LTS Market leasing is available exclusively for new equipment. Pre-owned devices are available through a separate sales offer.

How long does a leasing decision take?

The indicative processing time is one to three business days after the required documents have been submitted. Some applications may require additional processing time.

What types of leasing are available?

LTS Market assists customers with operating leases, finance leases and consumer leasing. Availability depends on the customer's circumstances and the financing institution's offer.

What documents are required?

Typical requirements include customer details, identification, a leasing application and an equipment quotation or pro forma invoice. Business applicants may also need to provide registration and financial documents.

Can a leasing agreement be terminated early?

Yes, early termination may be possible. The applicable conditions and any associated costs are determined by the leasing agreement and the financing institution's rules.

Does LTS Market assist with the application process?

Yes. We help customers prepare equipment documentation, explore available financing options and navigate the application process.

Is equipment training included?

Yes. Free equipment operation training is included when purchasing new equipment from LTS Market, including purchases financed through leasing.
