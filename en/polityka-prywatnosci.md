> Information about forms, external services and privacy at LTS Market.

## Data enquiries

Send personal data enquiries to sales@ltsmarket.pl. Only provide information needed to handle your enquiry.

## Contact forms

The forms currently have no sending service connected. Completing a form does not send a message. Please contact us by phone or email.

## External services

The site uses Google Fonts and embedded Google Maps. Loading these resources connects to the provider, which may receive your IP address and browser information.

## Analytics

Google Analytics starts automatically when you open the site and measures visits and site usage. The service uses cookies and sends Google information about activity on the site, the browser and the device.
